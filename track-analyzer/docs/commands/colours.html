Colours

The colors command learns how you colour-code tracks in your DJ app, checks your colours against it, explains what each colour means, and colours new tracks. Five actions: train, review, explain, apply and guessed.

Before you start

  • Run every command from the project folder, as uv run dj-track-analyzer colors <action>. See Running a command.
  • Scan your tracks first (scan --traktor): the colour model learns from the analysis of each track's sound.
  • Your colours are read from your DJ app: Traktor's collection.nml, or Rekordbox's library. Quit the app first so your latest changes are saved.
  • While a tempo view is loaded into Traktor, the colours there aren't yours, so these commands refuse. Switch back first with tempo native.
  • The colours are one scale, from Red to Magenta in Traktor's order. The model places each track on it (a position from 1 to 7) and the nearest colour is its guess.
  • Everything except explain prints its result as JSON.

On the web page, the same things live on the Colours tab and the Setup tab's Your colour scale card.

colors train

Learns your colour scale from the tracks you've already coloured, and saves it as your own colour model.

  • Fine with your DJ app open (but quit it so recent colours are saved)
  • Changes: the app's files only (data/my_colors.joblib)
  • Needs: 30 coloured tracks that are also scanned
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors train 2>&1 | tee logs/colors.txt
  • The first run listens to every scanned track again for the deeper features the model uses (MAEST and moods), so it takes a while. Later runs take about a minute.
  • Colours the app guessed itself, which nobody has checked yet, are left out (see guessed).
  • It prints how well the model does: how often it gets your colour exactly, how often within one colour, and the average distance in colours.
  • Until you train, the app uses a starter model learned from another DJ's colours. review needs your own.

Options

OptionWhat it doesDefault
--no-deepSkips the MAEST and mood features: faster, less accurate.off
--workers NHow many tracks to listen to at once in the deep pass.half the cores, at most 5
--refresh-viewsAfterwards, redoes every tempo view's colours with the new model, as tempo refresh does.off
--nml PATHReads this collection.nml instead of the one found automatically.found

Undo: nothing in your DJ app changes. Train again after recolouring to update the model.

colors review

Lists the tracks whose sound disagrees most with the colour you gave them. With --apply it recolours the clearest cases in your DJ app.

  • Read only without --apply
  • Quit your DJ app for --apply
  • Undo: traktor --undo
  • Needs: your own model (colors train)
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors review

It lists up to 25 tracks whose sound points at least 1.5 colours from yours, biggest gap first. Each track's guess comes from a model that hadn't seen that track's colour (cross-validation), so a track can't simply agree with itself. Per track:

{
 "file": "Artist - Title (Extended Mix).mp3",
 "your_colour": "Magenta",
 "sounds_like": "Yellow",
 "position": 2.78,
 "gap": 4.22,
 "confidence": 0.7,
 "confidence_exact": 0.26,
 "meets_apply_threshold": false,
 "kept": true,
 "path": "/path/to/Artist - Title (Extended Mix).mp3"
}
FieldMeaning
positionWhere the sound sits on the scale: 1 is Red, 7 is Magenta.
gapThe distance, in colours, between your colour and the guess.
confidenceHow often the tracks the model placed at this position really were that colour or a neighbour of it.
confidence_exactThe same, counting only exactly that colour.
meets_apply_thresholdWhether --apply would recolour it.
keptYou chose to keep its colour always, so --apply skips it.

Some will be the model's mistakes and some will be worth recolouring. Listen to the top ones in your DJ app before you decide.

Recolouring the clearest cases

A track qualifies when its gap is at least 2.5 colours and its confidence is at least 0.75. Preview first:

uv run dj-track-analyzer colors review --apply --dry-run

Then quit your DJ app and run it:

uv run dj-track-analyzer colors review --apply

It asks about each track:

  • y: recolour it
  • n: keep your colour
  • k: keep it and never ask about that track again
  • a: recolour all the rest
  • c: cancel without changing anything

Undo with traktor --undo before you open your DJ app again. Once Traktor has opened and saved the collection, the undo refuses unless you add --force, which loses what Traktor saved. Afterwards, train again so the model learns your corrections.

Options

OptionWhat it doesDefault
--applyRecolours the tracks over both thresholds in your DJ app (backup first).off
--dry-runWith --apply: lists what would be recoloured and changes nothing.off
--min-gap NWith --apply: only tracks at least this many colours from yours. Stricter: 3.2.5
--min-confidence NWith --apply: only tracks at or above this confidence (0–1). Stricter: 0.85.0.75
--yesWith --apply: recolours every qualifying track without asking.off
--track PATHWith --apply: recolours only this track, whatever the thresholds, without asking. Give the full path as the review lists it; repeat for several.none
--plannedWith --apply: writes what you marked on the web page's Colours tab. Approved tracks are recoloured, kept ones go on the keep-always list.off
--keptLists the tracks on the keep-always list instead of reviewing.off
--keep TEXTWith --kept: adds the coloured tracks whose path contains TEXT to the list.none
--forget TEXTWith --kept: takes tracks whose path contains TEXT off the list, so review suggests them again.none
--nml PATHUses this collection.nml.found

Examples:

uv run dj-track-analyzer colors review --apply --min-gap 3 --dry-run
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors review --kept --forget "part of the name"

colors explain

What each colour means in your collection: how the tracks sound, which playlists and sets they turn up in, and which artists.

  • Read only, fine with your DJ app open
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors explain

It prints tables, starting with the number of coloured tracks per colour. How to read them: Reading colors explain in the guide.

Options

OptionWhat it doesDefault
--jsonPrints JSON instead of tables.off
--workers NHow many tracks to listen to at once, for tracks still missing the deeper features.half the cores, at most 5
--nml PATHUses this collection.nml.found

colors apply

Analyses the tracks that are new in your DJ app and gives a colour to every track that has none. Colours you set are never changed, and genres and Smartlists are left alone.

  • Quit your DJ app (not for --dry-run)
  • Changes: your DJ app (backup first)
  • Undo: traktor --undo
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors apply --dry-run
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors apply
  • New tracks are analysed first, about 10–20 seconds each. The preview lists what it would set, e.g. Track.mp3: no colour → Orange.
  • Works before you train too, with the starter model. Your own model fits your colours better.
  • The colours it sets count as guesses until you check them: see guessed.
  • To colour and set genres in one go, use traktor or sync instead.

Options

OptionWhat it doesDefault
--dry-runLists which colours would be set and changes nothing.off
--min-confidence NOnly sets a colour when the model is at least this sure (0–1). Tracks below it stay uncoloured.0
--workers NHow many tracks to analyse at once.half the cores, at most 5
--nml PATHUses this collection.nml.found

colors guessed

Lists the colours the app wrote into your DJ app (by colors apply or traktor) that nobody has checked. train leaves them out, so the model doesn't learn from its own guesses.

  • Read only without --accept
  • With --accept: the app's database only
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors guessed

For each guess you can either:

  • change the colour in your DJ app, if it's wrong. It then counts as yours.
  • accept it, if it's right. train then learns from it.
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors guessed --accept "part of the name"
uv run dj-track-analyzer colors guessed --accept all

On the web page: the Tracks tab's All tracks → Colour guessed by the app, not checked yet, then Guessed colours are right.

Options

OptionWhat it doesDefault
--accept TEXTMarks the guesses whose path contains TEXT as right, at most 10 at a time so a short word can't accept half the collection. all accepts every one.none
--nml PATHUses this collection.nml.found
